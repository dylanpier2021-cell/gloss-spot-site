FAQ

Common questions

What events do you plan?

We plan birthdays, corporate events, weddings, baby showers, retirements, holiday parties, brand activations, and more. If you are planning a gathering of any size, contact us and we will tell you whether we are the right fit.

Do you serve outside Champaign County?

Our primary focus is Champaign County, IL. Reach out and describe your event location; we are happy to discuss options.

How far in advance should I book?

For corporate events and milestone birthdays, six to eight weeks gives us the best window to secure vendors and build a solid plan. Smaller gatherings can often be arranged in two to four weeks.

What is your pricing model?

Pricing depends on the size and scope of your event. Contact us for a custom quote and we will walk you through what is included.

Do you handle vendors?

Yes. Vendor research, coordination, and communication are part of the planning service. You will not need to chase anyone down on our watch.
