What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous process that removes surface imperfections from your vehicle's paintwork through machine polishing. This professional technique eliminates swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, and oxidation that dull your car's finish over time.

Unlike simple polishing or waxing, paint correction actually removes a microscopic layer of clear coat to reveal the fresh, pristine paint underneath. Our Savoy paint correction specialists use advanced compounds and techniques to restore your vehicle's original depth, clarity, and gloss.

The process requires expert knowledge of paint types, proper tool selection, and years of experience to achieve flawless results without causing damage. That's why Savoy vehicle owners trust The Gloss Spot for professional paint correction services.
