What is Engine Detailing?

Engine detailing is a specialized automotive service that involves the thorough cleaning, degreasing, and protection of your vehicle's engine bay. Our professional engine detailing service in Savoy goes far beyond a simple rinse – we meticulously clean every component, remove built-up grease and grime, and apply protective treatments to restore your engine bay to like-new condition.

The process involves careful preparation to protect sensitive electrical components, followed by the application of specialized degreasers and cleaners. We then perform detailed cleaning of all visible surfaces, hoses, covers, and components before applying protective treatments that help prevent future buildup while enhancing the engine bay's appearance. Professional engine detailing in Savoy not only improves aesthetics but can also help with engine cooling efficiency and make future maintenance easier by keeping components clean and accessible.

Our Engine Detailing Process

1

Preparation & Protection

We carefully cover and protect all sensitive electrical components, air intake systems, and electronic modules to ensure safe cleaning.

2

Degreasing Treatment

Application of professional-grade degreasers to break down built-up oil, grease, and grime on all engine bay surfaces and components.

3

Detailed Cleaning

Thorough cleaning of all accessible surfaces using appropriate brushes, tools, and techniques for different materials and components.

4

Rinse & Dry

Careful low-pressure rinsing followed by complete drying using compressed air and microfiber towels to prevent water spots.

5

Protection & Finishing

Application of appropriate protectants to plastic, rubber, and metal surfaces to restore shine and provide long-lasting protection.
