What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous process that removes imperfections from your vehicle's paint surface in Rantoul. Using specialized compounds, polishes, and techniques, we eliminate swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and other defects that dull your car's finish.

Unlike simple polishing or waxing, paint correction actually removes a microscopic layer of clear coat to level the surface and reveal the pristine paint underneath. This process requires professional-grade equipment, years of experience, and careful attention to detail.

Our paint correction services in Rantoul restore your vehicle's factory finish and often make it look better than new. The results are permanent improvements that dramatically enhance both appearance and resale value.

Our Paint Correction Process

1

Thorough Inspection & Wash

We begin with a complete paint inspection using specialized lighting to identify all defects. Your vehicle receives a thorough decontamination wash to remove all dirt, grime, and surface contaminants.

2

Paint Depth Measurement

Using professional paint thickness gauges, we measure your clear coat depth across the entire vehicle. This ensures safe correction without compromising the paint's integrity.

3

Multi-Stage Correction

We use progressively finer compounds and polishes to remove defects. Starting with cutting compounds for deep scratches, we finish with ultra-fine polishes for a mirror-like finish.

4

Final Inspection & Protection

Every panel receives a final inspection under multiple light sources. We then apply a high-quality protection product to preserve your newly corrected paint finish.

5

Quality Guarantee

We stand behind our work with a satisfaction guarantee. Your vehicle leaves our Rantoul facility with a finish that exceeds your expectations and maintains its beauty for years.
