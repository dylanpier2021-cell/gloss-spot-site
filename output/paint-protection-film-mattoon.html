What is Paint Protection Film?

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a premium transparent urethane material that provides an invisible shield for your vehicle's paint in Mattoon. Our professional PPF installation in Mattoon uses only the highest quality films that are virtually invisible once applied. The self-healing properties mean minor scratches disappear with heat, keeping your vehicle looking pristine year after year.
