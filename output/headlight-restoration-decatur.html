What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes oxidation, yellowing, and cloudiness from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, road debris, and environmental factors cause plastic headlight lenses to become foggy and discolored, dramatically reducing light output and compromising your safety on Decatur roads.

Our professional headlight restoration process uses specialized compounds and techniques to remove the damaged surface layer and restore crystal-clear clarity to your headlights. This service is significantly more affordable than headlight replacement and can improve light output by up to 300%, making your nighttime driving in Decatur much safer.

The process involves careful sanding with progressively finer grits, polishing with cutting compounds, and applying a protective UV-resistant coating to prevent future oxidation. The result is headlights that look and perform like new, often lasting several years with proper care.

Our Headlight Restoration Process

1

Inspection & Masking

We carefully inspect your headlights to assess the level of oxidation and damage, then thoroughly mask the surrounding areas to protect your vehicle's paint and trim during the restoration process.

2

Progressive Sanding

Using wet sanding techniques with progressively finer grits, we carefully remove the oxidized and damaged surface layer, working from coarse to fine to achieve optimal clarity.

3

Compound & Polish

We apply cutting compound to remove sanding marks, followed by polishing compound to achieve crystal-clear clarity and restore the headlight's optical properties.

4

UV Protection Coating

A high-quality UV-resistant coating is applied to protect against future oxidation and ensure your restored headlights maintain their clarity for years to come.

5

Final Inspection

We conduct a thorough quality inspection to ensure perfect results and provide you with care instructions to maintain your beautifully restored headlights.
