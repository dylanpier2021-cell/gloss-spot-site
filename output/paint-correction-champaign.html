What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction in Champaign is a meticulous multi-stage process that permanently removes surface defects from your vehicle's paint. Unlike traditional polishing that simply masks imperfections with fillers, our professional paint correction service eliminates swirl marks, scratches, water spots, oxidation, and other defects at the microscopic level.

Our Champaign paint correction specialists use precision techniques with various grades of compounds and polishes, working through progressively finer abrasives to restore your paint's original clarity and depth. This process reveals the true color and gloss potential of your vehicle's finish.

The result is a mirror-like finish that dramatically enhances your vehicle's appearance and value. Paint correction in Champaign is essential preparation for ceramic coatings and provides the foundation for long-lasting paint protection.

Our Paint Correction Process

1

Paint Evaluation & Wash

We thoroughly inspect your vehicle's paint condition and perform a comprehensive decontamination wash to prepare for correction in our Champaign facility.

2

Paint Thickness Measurement

Using professional paint depth gauges, we measure clear coat thickness across all panels to determine safe correction limits and appropriate techniques.

3

Multi-Stage Correction

We perform progressive correction stages using varying grades of compounds and polishes, working from heavy cutting to fine finishing for optimal results.

4

Final Inspection & Protection

Every panel is inspected under LED lighting to ensure perfection, followed by application of premium protection products for lasting results.

5

Quality Assurance

Final quality check and client walkthrough to ensure complete satisfaction with your Champaign paint correction service before delivery.
