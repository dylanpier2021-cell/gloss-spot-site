What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes the cloudy, yellowed, and oxidized layer from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, road debris, and environmental factors cause plastic headlight covers to become hazy and discolored, significantly reducing their effectiveness and your vehicle's appearance.

Our professional headlight restoration process in Champaign involves multiple steps of sanding, polishing, and sealing to restore your headlights to like-new clarity. This not only improves your vehicle's appearance but also enhances nighttime visibility and safety. The restoration process is much more cost-effective than replacing entire headlight assemblies, which can cost hundreds of dollars per headlight.

Professional headlight restoration typically lasts 2-3 years with proper care, making it an excellent investment for any vehicle owner in Champaign looking to improve both safety and aesthetics without the expense of new headlights.

Our Headlight Restoration Process

1

Inspection & Preparation

We thoroughly inspect your headlights to assess the level of oxidation and damage, then clean and mask the surrounding areas to protect your vehicle's paint.

2

Progressive Sanding

Using multiple grits of wet sandpaper, we carefully remove the oxidized layer, starting with coarser grits and progressing to finer ones for a smooth finish.

3

Polishing & Refinishing

We polish the headlights with specialized compounds to restore crystal clarity and remove any micro-scratches from the sanding process.

4

UV Protection Application

A high-quality UV-resistant coating is applied to protect against future oxidation and maintain clarity for years to come.

5

Final Inspection

We conduct a thorough quality check to ensure perfect clarity and light output, then test the headlights to verify optimal performance.
