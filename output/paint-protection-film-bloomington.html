    What is Paint Protection Film?

    Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a crystal-clear, virtually invisible urethane film that shields your vehicle's paint from road debris, stone chips, bug splatter, and minor scratches. In Bloomington's varied driving conditions, from highway gravel to seasonal weather challenges, PPF provides the ultimate defense for your investment.

    Our premium paint protection film is self-healing, meaning minor scratches and swirl marks disappear with heat exposure. This advanced technology keeps your vehicle looking showroom-new for years to come, preserving both its appearance and resale value.

    At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, we use only the highest quality films and precision installation techniques to ensure flawless coverage and long-lasting protection for your Bloomington vehicle.
