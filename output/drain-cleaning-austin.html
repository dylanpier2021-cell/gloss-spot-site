What is Drain Cleaning?

Professional drain cleaning is a comprehensive plumbing service that removes blockages, buildup, and debris from your pipes using specialized equipment and techniques. Unlike simple drain snaking or chemical cleaners, professional drain cleaning thoroughly clears your entire drainage system, from kitchen sinks to main sewer lines.

Our Austin drain cleaning experts use advanced tools like hydro-jetting, which employs high-pressure water streams to completely clear away grease, hair, soap scum, mineral deposits, and tree roots. This process not only eliminates current clogs but also removes the sticky residue that causes future blockages, leaving your pipes flowing like new.

Professional drain cleaning also includes video camera inspections to identify the exact location and cause of blockages, ensuring we address the root problem rather than just the symptoms. This thorough approach means longer-lasting results and fewer emergency calls, making it an essential maintenance service for Austin homeowners dealing with the area's unique plumbing challenges.
