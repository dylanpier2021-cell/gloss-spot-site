What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's paint, creating a protective layer that's incredibly durable and long-lasting. Unlike traditional waxes or sealants that sit on top of your paint, ceramic coating becomes part of your car's surface.

This advanced nanotechnology provides unmatched protection against UV rays, chemical stains, water spots, and environmental contaminants. In Kankakee's varying weather conditions, ceramic coating acts as a permanent shield that keeps your vehicle looking pristine year-round.

Our professional-grade ceramic coatings in Kankakee offer hydrophobic properties that make water bead and roll off your vehicle, reducing water spots and making maintenance significantly easier. The coating enhances your paint's gloss and depth while providing years of protection that traditional products simply can't match.

Our Ceramic Coating Process

1

Thorough Inspection & Wash

We begin with a detailed inspection of your vehicle's paint condition, followed by a thorough wash to remove all surface contaminants and prepare for coating application.

2

Paint Correction & Polishing

Any paint imperfections, swirl marks, or scratches are corrected through professional polishing to ensure a flawless surface for the ceramic coating.

3

Surface Decontamination

We perform a clay bar treatment and chemical decontamination to remove embedded contaminants that washing alone cannot eliminate.

4

Professional Application

The ceramic coating is meticulously applied in controlled conditions using professional techniques to ensure even coverage and optimal bonding.

5

Curing & Quality Inspection

The coating is allowed to cure properly, followed by a comprehensive quality inspection to ensure perfect application and coverage.
