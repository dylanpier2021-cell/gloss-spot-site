What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes the cloudy, yellow, and oxidized layer from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, road debris, and environmental factors cause plastic headlight lenses to become foggy and discolored, significantly reducing their effectiveness and your vehicle's safety.

Our professional headlight restoration process in Monticello uses advanced techniques and high-quality materials to remove oxidation, restore clarity, and apply protective coatings that help prevent future damage. This service not only improves your vehicle's appearance but also enhances visibility and safety during night driving, ensuring you meet Illinois safety inspection requirements.

Our Headlight Restoration Process

1

Inspection & Assessment

We thoroughly examine your headlights to determine the extent of oxidation and damage, ensuring the right restoration approach for your Monticello vehicle.

2

Surface Preparation

Careful cleaning and masking of surrounding areas to protect your vehicle's paint while preparing the headlight surface for restoration.

3

Multi-Stage Sanding

Progressive sanding with increasingly fine grits to remove oxidation, scratches, and cloudiness while maintaining the headlight's structural integrity.

4

Polishing & Restoration

Professional polishing compounds restore crystal clarity and remove any remaining haze, bringing back the headlight's original transparency.

5

Protective Coating

Application of premium UV-resistant coating to protect against future oxidation and extend the life of your restoration in Monticello's climate.
