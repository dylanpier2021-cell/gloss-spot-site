What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous process that removes surface imperfections from your vehicle's paint, including swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and other defects that dull your car's appearance. Using advanced polishing compounds and techniques, our Mahomet paint correction specialists carefully level the paint surface to restore its original depth, clarity, and shine.
Unlike simple polishing, true paint correction involves removing microscopic layers of clear coat to eliminate defects completely. This process requires skill, experience, and professional-grade equipment to achieve flawless results without damaging the paint. Our paint correction services in Mahomet transform weathered, scratched vehicles back to showroom condition.

Our Paint Correction Process

1

Thorough Inspection

We carefully examine your vehicle's paint under proper lighting to identify all scratches, swirl marks, and defects. This assessment determines the level of correction needed for your Mahomet paint correction service.

2

Paint Depth Measurement

Using precision gauges, we measure paint thickness across all panels to ensure safe correction without damaging the clear coat. This step is crucial for achieving optimal results.

3

Multi-Stage Correction

We perform progressive polishing stages, starting with cutting compounds to remove defects, then refining with finer polishes to achieve perfect clarity and depth.

4

Final Inspection & Protection

After correction, we inspect the results under multiple light sources and apply protective products to maintain your newly corrected paint finish.
