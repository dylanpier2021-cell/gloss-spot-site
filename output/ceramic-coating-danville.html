What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's paint, creating a permanent protective layer. Unlike traditional waxes or sealants, ceramic coating provides long-lasting protection that can last for years with proper maintenance.

At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing in Danville, we use only premium ceramic coating products that have been tested to withstand Illinois weather conditions. Our professional application process ensures optimal bonding and maximum durability for your vehicle's protection.

The nano-ceramic technology creates a hydrophobic surface that repels water, dirt, and contaminants while providing UV protection and enhanced gloss. This means your car stays cleaner longer and maintains that showroom shine throughout Danville's changing seasons.

Our Ceramic Coating Process

1

Thorough Inspection & Wash

We begin with a comprehensive inspection of your vehicle's paint condition, followed by a deep wash to remove all surface contaminants.

2

Paint Decontamination

Clay bar treatment and chemical decontamination remove embedded particles that regular washing can't eliminate, ensuring perfect surface preparation.

3

Paint Correction (If Needed)

Any swirl marks, scratches, or imperfections are corrected through our professional polishing process to achieve a flawless finish.

4

Ceramic Coating Application

The ceramic coating is carefully applied panel by panel in a controlled environment, ensuring even coverage and proper bonding.

5

Curing & Final Inspection

The coating is allowed to cure properly, followed by a final quality inspection to ensure perfect results before your vehicle leaves our Danville facility.
