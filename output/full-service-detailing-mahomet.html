What is Full Service Detailing?

Full service detailing is the most comprehensive car care service available in Mahomet, combining both interior and exterior detailing into one complete package. This premium service includes thorough exterior washing, clay bar treatment, polishing, and protective waxing, along with complete interior deep cleaning, leather conditioning, and dashboard restoration.

At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, our Mahomet full service detailing goes beyond basic car washes.
