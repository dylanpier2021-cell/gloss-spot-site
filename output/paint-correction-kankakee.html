What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous multi-step process that removes surface imperfections from your vehicle's paintwork. Through the controlled use of specialized compounds and polishes, we permanently eliminate swirl marks, fine scratches, water spots, oxidation, and other blemishes that dull your car's finish.

Unlike traditional waxing or polishing that simply masks defects, paint correction actually removes microscopic layers of clear coat to reveal the pristine paint underneath. This process requires professional-grade equipment, extensive training, and years of experience to achieve flawless results without compromising your vehicle's protective coating.

For Kankakee vehicle owners dealing with harsh winter conditions, road salt exposure, and summer sun damage, paint correction is essential for maintaining your car's appearance and resale value. Our professional paint correction services in Kankakee restore that showroom shine you thought was gone forever.

Our Paint Correction Process

1

Paint Assessment & Preparation

We thoroughly inspect your vehicle's paint using specialized lighting to identify all defects. The car is then carefully washed and decontaminated to remove all surface contaminants before correction begins.

2

Paint Thickness Measurement

Using precision digital gauges, we measure paint thickness across your entire vehicle to ensure safe correction levels and prevent damage to the underlying primer or metal.

3

Multi-Stage Correction Process

We perform progressive correction stages using cutting compounds and polishing agents, removing deeper scratches first, then refining to eliminate micro-marring and achieve maximum gloss.

4

Protection & Finishing

After correction is complete, we apply premium protection in the form of wax, sealant, or ceramic coating to preserve your newly perfected finish and maintain that showroom shine.
