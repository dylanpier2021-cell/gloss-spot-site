What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous automotive detailing process that permanently removes surface imperfections from your vehicle's paintwork. Unlike polishing or waxing, which temporarily masks defects, paint correction actually eliminates swirl marks, light scratches, oxidation, water spots, and other paint defects through a systematic multi-stage process.

Our paint correction process uses specialized compounds and polishes applied with precision equipment to level the paint surface and restore optical clarity. This reveals the true depth and gloss of your vehicle's paint, often making it look better than when it first left the factory. For Urbana vehicle owners dealing with harsh winters, road salt damage, and intense summer UV exposure, paint correction is essential for maintaining your car's value and appearance.

Professional paint correction requires extensive training, high-quality tools, and years of experience to achieve flawless results without damaging your vehicle's clear coat. At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, we've perfected our paint correction techniques specifically for the challenging conditions that Urbana vehicles face throughout the year.

Our Paint Correction Process

1

Paint Assessment

We thoroughly inspect your vehicle's paint using specialized lighting to identify all defects and determine the appropriate correction level for your Urbana vehicle.

2

Decontamination

Complete wash, clay bar treatment, and chemical decontamination to remove all surface contaminants, preparing the paint for the correction process.

3

Multi-Stage Correction

Using professional-grade compounds and polishes, we systematically remove defects through multiple stages, monitoring progress with paint depth gauges.

4

Final Refinement

Fine polishing to achieve maximum gloss and optical clarity, followed by thorough inspection under multiple light sources to ensure perfection.

5

Protection Application

Application of premium wax, sealant, or ceramic coating to protect your newly corrected paint from Urbana's environmental challenges.
