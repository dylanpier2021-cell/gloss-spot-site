What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied to your vehicle's exterior that chemically bonds with the factory paint, creating a protective layer. This advanced nanotechnology provides superior protection against environmental contaminants, UV rays, bird droppings, tree sap, and everyday wear and tear.

Unlike traditional waxes that sit on top of your paint, ceramic coatings form a permanent bond that can last years. The result is a glossy, hydrophobic surface that repels water, makes cleaning easier, and maintains that showroom shine longer than any other paint protection method.

In Champaign's climate, with harsh winters and intense summer sun, ceramic coating provides essential protection that keeps your vehicle looking pristine year-round. Our professional-grade ceramic coatings offer durability and performance that far exceeds what's available to consumers.
