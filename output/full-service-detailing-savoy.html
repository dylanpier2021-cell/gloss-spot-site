What is Full Service Detailing?

Full service auto detailing in Savoy, IL is the most comprehensive car care service available. It combines both interior and exterior detailing into one complete package that addresses every aspect of your vehicle's appearance and condition.

Our full service detailing includes thorough exterior washing, clay bar treatment, polishing, waxing, and paint protection. Inside your vehicle, we perform deep cleaning of all surfaces, leather conditioning, carpet shampooing, and complete interior protection. This comprehensive approach ensures your vehicle looks and feels like new.

Unlike basic car washes, full service detailing is a meticulous process that can take 4-6 hours to complete. Every surface is carefully cleaned, treated, and protected using professional-grade products and techniques. The result is a vehicle that not only looks stunning but is also protected against future wear and environmental damage.
