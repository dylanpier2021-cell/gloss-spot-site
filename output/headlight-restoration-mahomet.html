What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes the cloudy, yellowed, and oxidized layer from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, weather, and road debris cause plastic headlight lenses to become foggy and discolored, significantly reducing light output and visibility.

Our headlight restoration process in Mahomet uses specialized equipment and multi-step sanding techniques to completely remove the damaged outer layer, revealing the clear plastic underneath. We then apply UV-resistant sealants to protect against future oxidation.

This service is perfect for Mahomet drivers whose headlights have become dim, yellowed, or cloudy. Professional restoration not only improves visibility and safety but also enhances your vehicle's appearance and can even increase resale value.
