What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes oxidation, yellowing, and cloudiness from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, headlights become foggy and dim due to UV exposure, road debris, and environmental factors. Our expert car detailing services in Normal include comprehensive headlight restoration that not only improves your vehicle's appearance but also significantly enhances safety by restoring optimal light output.

The process involves carefully sanding away the damaged surface layer, polishing the lens to restore clarity, and applying a protective UV coating to prevent future damage. Professional headlight restoration in Normal is far more effective than DIY kits, providing lasting results that keep your headlights clear for years to come.
