What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's factory paint to create a permanent protective layer. Unlike traditional wax or sealants, ceramic coating provides superior protection against UV rays, chemicals, bird droppings, and environmental contaminants that can damage your car's finish.

Our professional ceramic coating services in Normal use only the highest quality coatings that provide years of protection while maintaining that showroom shine. The hydrophobic properties make water and dirt slide right off, making your vehicle easier to clean and maintain. Our Ceramic Coating Process

1

Thorough Inspection & Wash

We start with a complete vehicle inspection and multi-stage wash to remove all contaminants and prepare your car's surface for coating application.

2

Paint Correction & Decontamination

Any paint defects, swirl marks, or scratches are corrected using professional polishing techniques. We then perform clay bar decontamination for a perfectly smooth surface.

3

Surface Preparation

The paint is wiped down with isopropyl alcohol to remove any remaining oils or residues, ensuring maximum bonding for the ceramic coating.

4

Ceramic Coating Application

Our certified technicians apply the ceramic coating in controlled sections, working methodically to ensure even coverage and proper bonding to your vehicle's paint.

5

Curing & Final Inspection

The coating is allowed to cure in our controlled environment. We perform a final inspection and quality check before delivering your protected vehicle back to you in Normal.
