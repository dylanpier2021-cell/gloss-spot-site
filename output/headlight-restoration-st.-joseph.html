    What is Headlight Restoration?

    Headlight restoration is a professional automotive service that removes oxidation, yellowing, and cloudiness from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, road debris, and environmental factors cause plastic headlight covers to become foggy and discolored, significantly reducing light output and compromising safety.

    Our professional headlight restoration process in St. Joseph involves carefully sanding away the damaged outer layer, polishing the lens to crystal clarity, and applying a protective coating to prevent future oxidation. This service restores your headlights to like-new condition, dramatically improving visibility and enhancing your vehicle's appearance.

    Rather than replacing expensive headlight assemblies, restoration offers a cost-effective solution that can improve light output by up to 200%. For St. Joseph drivers dealing with cloudy or yellowed headlights, professional restoration is the smart choice for safety, aesthetics, and value.
