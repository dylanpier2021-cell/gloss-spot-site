What is Paint Protection Film?

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a clear, durable thermoplastic urethane film that protects your vehicle's paint from chips, scratches, swirl marks, and environmental damage. Also known as clear bra, PPF is virtually invisible once applied and maintains your car's original appearance while providing superior protection. Our expert team in Danville uses only premium-grade films that offer self-healing properties, meaning minor scratches disappear with heat exposure. Whether you drive through Danville's busy streets or venture on highway trips, Paint Protection Film ensures your vehicle maintains its showroom finish for years to come.
