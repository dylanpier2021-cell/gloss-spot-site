What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration in Rantoul is a professional service that restores cloudy, yellowed, or oxidized headlight lenses to their original crystal-clear condition. Our professional headlight restoration process uses specialized compounds, polishes, and UV-protective sealants to remove oxidation, scratches, and discoloration.
