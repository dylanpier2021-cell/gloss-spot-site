What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is the process of removing imperfections from your vehicle's paint surface through careful cutting, polishing, and refining techniques. Unlike regular detailing, paint correction actually removes defects rather than just hiding them.

Our paint correction services in Danville address swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, oxidation, and other paint defects that dull your car's appearance. Using professional-grade compounds and polishes, we restore your paint to its original clarity and depth.

The process involves multiple stages of cutting and polishing, with each stage using progressively finer compounds to remove defects and enhance gloss. The result is paint that looks better than new, with incredible depth and mirror-like clarity.
