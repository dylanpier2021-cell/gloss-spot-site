What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes the cloudy, yellowed oxidation from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, road chemicals, and environmental factors cause plastic headlight covers to become hazy and discolored, dramatically reducing light output and compromising safety.

Our professional headlight restoration process in Urbana uses specialized compounds and techniques to remove oxidation, restore clarity, and apply protective coatings that help prevent future clouding. The result is dramatically improved visibility, enhanced vehicle appearance, and restored safety for night driving in Urbana and surrounding areas.

Professional headlight restoration is significantly more cost-effective than replacing headlight assemblies, which can cost hundreds or even thousands of dollars. Our restoration service provides like-new results at a fraction of the replacement cost, making it an excellent investment for Urbana vehicle owners.
