    What is Paint Correction?

    Paint correction is a meticulous process that removes imperfections from your vehicle's paint surface. Unlike simple polishing or waxing, paint correction actually eliminates swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and other defects that dull your car's finish.

    Our St. Joseph paint correction specialists use professional-grade compounds and polishes to restore your vehicle's paint to its original luster. This multi-step process involves careful assessment, proper preparation, and precise application of corrective products to achieve showroom-quality results.

    The result is a mirror-like finish that not only looks stunning but also provides better protection for your vehicle's paint. Paint correction is essential preparation before applying ceramic coating in St. Joseph or other protective treatments.
