What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes the cloudy, yellowed, and oxidized layer from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, road debris, and environmental contaminants cause headlights to become hazy and dim, significantly reducing visibility and compromising safety. Our expert technicians in Savoy use specialized compounds, polishing techniques, and protective coatings to restore your headlights to like-new clarity.

The process involves careful sanding, polishing, and sealing to not only improve appearance but also restore optimal light output. Professional headlight restoration in Savoy can improve your visibility by up to 50% while adding significant value to your vehicle. It's a cost-effective alternative to expensive headlight replacement that delivers exceptional results.

Our Headlight Restoration Process

1

Inspection & Assessment

We thoroughly examine your headlights to determine the level of oxidation and damage, then explain the restoration process and expected results.

2

Surface Preparation

We carefully mask surrounding areas and begin with wet sanding using progressively finer grits to remove the oxidized layer and surface imperfections.

3

Polishing & Refinishing

Using specialized polishing compounds and techniques, we restore optical clarity and remove all haze, scratches, and discoloration from the headlight lens.

4

Protective Coating

We apply a premium UV-resistant clear coat to protect against future oxidation and maintain the restored clarity for years to come.

5

Final Inspection

We conduct a thorough quality check to ensure perfect clarity and light transmission, then provide care instructions to maintain your restored headlights.
