What is Interior Detailing?

Interior detailing is a comprehensive deep cleaning and restoration process that goes far beyond a simple car wash or vacuum. At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, our professional interior detailing service in St. Joseph transforms your vehicle's cabin into a pristine, fresh environment.

Our interior detailing process includes thorough vacuuming of all surfaces, steam cleaning of carpets and upholstery, leather conditioning, dashboard and trim cleaning, window cleaning from the inside, odor elimination, and stain removal. We use professional-grade equipment and products to ensure every surface in your vehicle is properly cleaned, conditioned, and protected.

Unlike basic cleaning services, our interior detailing in St. Joseph focuses on restoration and protection. We treat different materials appropriately - leather surfaces receive conditioning treatments, plastic and vinyl are cleaned and protected with UV protectants, and fabric surfaces are steam cleaned and deodorized. The result is a vehicle interior that looks, feels, and smells like new.
