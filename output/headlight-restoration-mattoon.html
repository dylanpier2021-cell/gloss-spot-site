What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that removes the yellowing, cloudiness, and oxidation from your vehicle's headlight lenses. Over time, UV rays, road debris, and weather conditions cause plastic headlight covers to become foggy and discolored, significantly reducing their light output and compromising your safety on Mattoon roads.

Our professional headlight restoration process uses specialized tools and compounds to sand away the damaged outer layer, polish the surface to crystal clarity, and apply a protective coating that prevents future oxidation. The result is headlights that look and perform like new, dramatically improving your vehicle's appearance and your driving safety in Mattoon.

Most Mattoon drivers are amazed by the transformation – what looked like expensive headlight replacement becomes an affordable restoration that can improve light output by up to 300%.
