What is Exterior Detailing?

Exterior detailing is a comprehensive cleaning, restoration, and protection process for your vehicle's exterior surfaces. Unlike a basic car wash, professional exterior detailing in Mahomet goes deep to remove embedded contaminants, correct paint imperfections, and apply protective coatings that keep your car looking showroom fresh.

Our exterior detailing process includes thorough washing, clay bar treatment to remove bonded contaminants, paint correction to eliminate swirl marks and light scratches, and application of premium wax or ceramic coating for long-lasting protection. We also detail wheels, tires, chrome, and glass to ensure every exterior surface shines.

For Mahomet vehicle owners, regular exterior detailing is essential protection against harsh Illinois weather, road salt, UV rays, and environmental contaminants that can permanently damage your car's paint and finish.

1

Pre-Wash Inspection
