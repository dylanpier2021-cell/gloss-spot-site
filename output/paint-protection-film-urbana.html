What is Paint Protection Film?

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a clear, thermoplastic urethane film applied to painted surfaces of vehicles to protect them from stone chips, bug splatters, and minor abrasions. This invisible shield preserves your vehicle's original paint while maintaining its factory appearance.

Our professional-grade PPF features self-healing properties that eliminate minor scratches and swirl marks when exposed to heat. The film is optically clear, maintaining your vehicle's original color and finish while providing long-lasting protection against road debris, weather damage, and UV rays.
