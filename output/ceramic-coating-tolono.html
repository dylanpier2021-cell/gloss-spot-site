What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's paint, creating a protective layer that lasts for years. This advanced nanotechnology provides superior protection against UV rays, oxidation, chemical stains, and environmental contaminants that can damage your vehicle's finish.

Unlike traditional wax or sealants that wear off in weeks, ceramic coating in Tolono creates a semi-permanent bond that maintains its protective properties for 3-5 years. The coating forms a hydrophobic surface that causes water and dirt to bead and roll off, making maintenance easier while preserving that showroom shine.

Our professional-grade ceramic coatings used in Tolono provide 9H hardness protection, meaning they're nearly as hard as glass. This creates an invisible shield that protects against minor scratches, swirl marks, and environmental damage while enhancing your vehicle's gloss and depth of color.
