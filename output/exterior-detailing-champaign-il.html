Our Exterior Detailing Process

Every exterior detailing service in Champaign follows our proven five-step process, ensuring consistent, premium results every time.

1

Pre-Wash & Inspection

We thoroughly rinse your vehicle and perform a detailed inspection to identify paint imperfections, contamination levels, and areas requiring special attention.

2

Deep Cleaning & Decontamination

Using premium soaps and the two-bucket method, we safely remove dirt and grime. Clay bar treatment eliminates embedded contaminants that washing alone cannot remove.

3

Paint Correction

We address swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation using professional-grade compounds and polishes, restoring your paint's clarity and depth.

4

Protection Application

Premium wax, sealant, or ceramic coating is applied to create a protective barrier against UV rays, contaminants, and weather damage.

5

Final Details & Inspection

We clean and dress all exterior trim, wheels, and glass, followed by a comprehensive quality inspection to ensure perfection.
