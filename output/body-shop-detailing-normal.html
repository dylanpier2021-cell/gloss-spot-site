What is Body Shop Detailing?

Body shop detailing in Normal is a specialized service that bridges the gap between collision repair and final delivery. Our expert technicians provide comprehensive paint correction, surface preparation, and finishing services specifically designed for body shops and collision centers throughout Normal and surrounding areas.

Unlike regular car detailing services in Normal, body shop detailing focuses on paint matching, surface smoothing, removing orange peel texture, eliminating dust nibs, and applying protective coatings to ensure repaired vehicles meet or exceed factory finish standards.

Our Normal body shop detailing services include wet sanding, compounding, polishing, paint correction, ceramic coating application, and final quality inspection. We work with insurance companies, dealerships, and independent body shops to deliver consistent, professional results that satisfy customers and meet industry standards.
