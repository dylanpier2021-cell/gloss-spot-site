What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous multi-stage process that removes imperfections from your vehicle's paint surface. Unlike simple polishing, true paint correction involves permanently removing defects like swirl marks, scratches, water spots, and oxidation through controlled abrasion techniques.

Our professional paint correction service in Champaign uses advanced compounds, polishes, and cutting-edge equipment to restore your vehicle's paint to its original factory finish. The process reveals the true depth and clarity of your car's paint, often making it look better than when it left the showroom.

Paint correction is essential for maintaining your vehicle's appearance and protecting its resale value. Champaign's harsh winter conditions, road salt, and everyday driving can take a toll on your car's finish. Our expert technicians assess each vehicle individually to determine the appropriate correction level needed.
