What is Exterior Detailing?

Exterior detailing is a comprehensive cleaning and restoration process that focuses on the outside of your vehicle. Our professional exterior detailing services in Danville go far beyond a basic car wash. We meticulously clean, polish, and protect every exterior surface of your vehicle.

The process includes thorough washing, clay bar treatment to remove embedded contaminants, polishing to restore shine, waxing or sealant application for protection, and detailed cleaning of wheels, tires, and trim. Our exterior detailing services in Danville restore your vehicle's paint to like-new condition while providing long-lasting protection against the elements.

We use professional-grade products and techniques that aren't available with standard car washes. From paint correction to ceramic coating application, our car detailing services in Danville are designed to enhance and preserve your vehicle's exterior beauty.

Our Exterior Detailing Process

1

Thorough Pre-Wash

We begin by rinsing your vehicle and applying pre-wash treatments to loosen dirt and grime. This prevents scratching during the washing process.

2

Hand Washing & Clay Bar

Your vehicle is carefully hand washed using the two-bucket method, followed by clay bar treatment to remove embedded contaminants from the paint surface.

3

Paint Correction & Polishing

We assess your paint condition and perform necessary correction work, removing swirl marks and light scratches, then polish to restore optimal shine.

4

Protection Application

High-quality wax, sealant, or ceramic coating is applied to protect your paint and enhance gloss. We also treat trim, tires, and wheels.

5

Final Inspection

We perform a comprehensive inspection and final detailing touches to ensure every surface meets our high standards before delivery.
