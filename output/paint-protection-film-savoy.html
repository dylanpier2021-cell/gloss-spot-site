What is Paint Protection Film?

Paint protection film (PPF) is a clear, thermoplastic urethane film that provides an invisible shield for your vehicle's paint. This advanced material was originally developed for military use to protect helicopter blades from debris damage, and has since been adapted for automotive applications.

Modern paint protection film is virtually invisible when properly installed, maintaining your vehicle's original appearance while providing superior protection against rock chips, scratches, bird droppings, tree sap, and UV damage. The film features self-healing properties that allow minor scratches to disappear with heat application.

Our Savoy paint protection film installation process ensures perfect alignment and bubble-free application. We use only premium film brands with manufacturer warranties, giving you peace of mind that your investment is protected for years to come.
