What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous process that removes surface defects from your vehicle's paint. Using specialized tools and compounds, we eliminate swirl marks, scratches, water spots, and oxidation that dull your car's finish. This multi-stage polishing process restores your paint to its original depth and clarity, creating a mirror-like finish that looks better than new.

Our professional paint correction service in Monticello goes beyond simple polishing. We carefully assess your vehicle's paint condition, then use progressive abrasives to level the clear coat and remove defects. The result is a flawless finish that enhances your vehicle's appearance and protects its value. Whether you're dealing with years of washing damage or specific scratches, our car detailing services in Monticello can restore your paint's original beauty.
