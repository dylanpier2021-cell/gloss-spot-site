Our Exterior Detailing Process

1

Pre-Wash Inspection & Rinse

We thoroughly inspect your vehicle and perform a complete rinse to remove loose dirt and debris before beginning the detailed cleaning process.

2

Multi-Step Washing

Using the two-bucket method and premium microfiber materials, we wash your vehicle from top to bottom with pH-neutral shampoos that won't strip existing protection.

3

Clay Bar Decontamination

We use professional clay bars to remove embedded contaminants, tar, and other particles that regular washing cannot eliminate, leaving your paint smooth and clean.

4

Paint Correction & Polishing

Using machine polishers and cutting compounds, we correct paint imperfections, remove swirl marks, and restore depth and clarity to your vehicle's finish.

5

Protection & Final Details

We apply premium wax or paint sealant for long-lasting protection, then detail wheels, tires, and trim for a complete, showroom-quality finish.
