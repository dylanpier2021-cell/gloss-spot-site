FAQ

Questions people ask

How long does ceramic coating last?

We offer 3-year, 6-year and 10-year coatings. Regular hand washes keep it performing its best for the full term.

How much is ceramic coating in Champaign?

A 3-year coating with 1-step correction is $1,100 for a coupe, $1,350 for a sedan or small truck, $1,600 for a small SUV or medium truck and $1,850 for a large SUV, truck or minivan. Book online and see your exact price first.

Is paint correction included with ceramic coating?

Yes. Every ceramic package includes paint correction, so the coating locks in a corrected finish.

Is ceramic coating better than wax?

Wax lasts weeks. Ceramic bonds to your paint and lasts years, with easier washes the whole time.

How long will you need my car?

The whole day. Drop it off in the morning and we keep it for correction, coating and cure. We'll text you when it's ready.

When can I wash my car after ceramic coating?

We'll give you the cure time at pickup. After that, a pH-neutral hand wash keeps it slick.
