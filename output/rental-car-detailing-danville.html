What is Rental Car Detailing?

Rental car detailing is a specialized automotive cleaning service designed specifically for rental vehicle fleets in Danville, IL. Our Danville rental car detailing service includes deep cleaning of upholstery, dashboard restoration, thorough vacuuming, exterior washing and waxing, window cleaning, and complete vehicle sanitization. We understand the unique challenges rental car companies face with high turnover rates and varying levels of vehicle condition.
