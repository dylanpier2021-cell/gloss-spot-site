What is Fleet Detailing?

Fleet detailing is a specialized service designed to maintain and enhance the appearance of multiple business vehicles. Unlike standard car detailing, fleet detailing focuses on efficiency, consistency, and cost-effectiveness while maintaining professional standards. Our Urbana fleet detailing service handles everything from small business vehicle fleets to large corporate transportation departments.

We understand that your fleet represents your business image on Urbana roads every day. Our comprehensive fleet detailing service includes exterior washing, interior cleaning, window cleaning, tire shine, and optional protective treatments. We work with delivery companies, service businesses, rental agencies, and corporate fleets throughout Urbana to keep their vehicles looking professional and well-maintained.

The process is tailored to your specific needs and schedule, with options for on-site service at your Urbana location or scheduled visits to our facility. We use commercial-grade products and efficient techniques to deliver consistent results across your entire fleet while minimizing downtime for your business operations.
