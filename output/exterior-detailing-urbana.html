What is Exterior Detailing?

Exterior detailing is a comprehensive cleaning and restoration process that goes far beyond a basic car wash. Our Urbana exterior detailing service includes thorough paint decontamination, clay bar treatment, paint correction to remove swirl marks and scratches, followed by protective wax or ceramic coating application.

The process begins with a gentle hand wash using pH-balanced soaps, followed by clay bar treatment to remove embedded contaminants that regular washing can't eliminate. We then perform paint correction using professional-grade compounds and polishes to restore your paint's clarity and depth.

Finally, we apply premium protection through high-quality wax, paint sealants, or ceramic coatings to shield your vehicle from Urbana's harsh weather conditions, UV rays, and environmental pollutants. This multi-step process restores your vehicle's showroom shine while providing long-lasting protection.
