What is Headlight Restoration?

Headlight restoration is a professional service that brings cloudy, yellowed, or oxidized headlights back to their original clarity and brightness. Over time, your vehicle's headlights develop a hazy film due to UV exposure, road debris, and environmental factors. This not only affects your car's appearance but significantly reduces visibility and safety while driving, especially at night.

Our professional headlight restoration process in Bloomington removes oxidation, scratches, and discoloration through a multi-step sanding, polishing, and protective coating system. This service restores up to 90% of your headlights' original brightness while protecting them from future UV damage. The result is dramatically improved visibility, enhanced safety, and a like-new appearance that can increase your vehicle's value.

Whether your headlights are slightly hazy or severely yellowed, our expert technicians have the tools and experience to restore them to optimal condition. We serve Bloomington and surrounding areas with mobile convenience, bringing our professional restoration service directly to your location.

Our Headlight Restoration Process

1

Assessment & Preparation

We thoroughly inspect your headlights to determine the restoration approach needed and carefully mask surrounding areas to protect your vehicle's paint.

2

Deep Cleaning & Sanding

Using progressive grits of wet sandpaper, we remove the oxidized layer and deep scratches, working from coarse to fine for optimal results.

3

Professional Polishing

We use specialized compounds and polishing equipment to restore crystal-clear transparency and remove all sanding marks from the surface.

4

UV Protection Application

A professional-grade protective coating is applied to prevent future oxidation and maintain clarity for years to come.

5

Final Inspection

We conduct a thorough quality check and provide maintenance tips to ensure your restored headlights stay clear and bright.
