What is Body Shop Detailing?

Body shop detailing in Springfield, IL is a specialized service designed to help auto body repair facilities deliver vehicles in pristine condition to their customers. Our professional body shop detailing goes far beyond basic cleaning—we provide comprehensive paint correction, scratch removal, and restoration services that ensure every vehicle leaving your Springfield body shop meets the highest standards of appearance and quality.

At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, we understand the unique challenges Springfield body shops face. Our Body Shop Detailing Process

01

Vehicle Inspection & Assessment

We thoroughly evaluate each vehicle at your Springfield body shop to identify specific detailing needs and create a customized service plan.

02

Paint Decontamination

Our team removes all compound residue, overspray, and contaminants from the vehicle's surface using specialized clay bar and decontamination techniques.

03

Multi-Stage Paint Correction

Interior Restoration

Final Protection & Delivery
