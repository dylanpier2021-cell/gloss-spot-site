What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous process that removes surface defects from your vehicle's paintwork through controlled abrasion. Our Bloomington paint correction specialists use professional-grade compounds and polishes to eliminate swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and other imperfections that dull your car's finish.

Unlike basic polishing, paint correction in Bloomington requires expertise to assess your vehicle's paint thickness, select appropriate compounds, and execute multi-stage correction safely. The result is a mirror-like finish that restores your vehicle's original luster and prepares it for long-term protection like ceramic coating.

Our Bloomington team has perfected paint correction techniques for all vehicle types, from daily drivers to exotic sports cars. We understand local conditions that affect paint health and tailor our approach accordingly.

Our Paint Correction Process

1

Thorough Inspection & Assessment

We evaluate your vehicle's paint condition using specialized lighting and paint thickness gauges to develop a customized correction plan for your Bloomington vehicle.

2

Deep Clean & Decontamination

Complete wash, clay bar treatment, and iron removal ensure the cleanest possible surface before correction begins. This step is crucial for optimal results.

3

Multi-Stage Correction

Using progressive compounds and polishes, we methodically remove defects while preserving paint thickness. Each section receives individual attention for uniform results.

4

Final Polish & Protection

A jeweling polish creates the final mirror finish, followed by your choice of wax, sealant, or ceramic coating to lock in the correction.

5

Quality Inspection

Every panel is inspected under multiple light sources to ensure flawless results before we deliver your beautifully corrected Bloomington vehicle.
