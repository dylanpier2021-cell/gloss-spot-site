What is Interior Detailing?

Interior detailing is a comprehensive deep cleaning and restoration service that goes far beyond a simple car wash. At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, we specialize in transforming your vehicle's interior from top to bottom. Our professional interior detailing service in Kankakee includes thorough vacuuming of all surfaces, deep cleaning of upholstery and carpets, leather conditioning, dashboard and console cleaning, window cleaning, and protective treatments.

Unlike basic car cleaning, interior detailing addresses every nook and cranny of your vehicle's interior. We use specialized equipment like steam cleaners, extractors, and professional-grade cleaning products to remove stains, odors, dirt, and grime that regular cleaning can't reach. Our Kankakee interior detailing service restores your vehicle's interior to like-new condition while protecting surfaces from future wear and damage.

Whether you drive a sedan, SUV, truck, or luxury vehicle, our interior detailing service in Kankakee is customized to meet your specific needs and your vehicle's requirements.

Our Interior Detailing Process

1

Complete Interior Assessment

We begin by thoroughly inspecting your vehicle's interior condition, identifying stains, wear patterns, and specific areas that need special attention in your Kankakee vehicle.

2

Deep Vacuuming & Debris Removal

Using professional-grade equipment, we remove all debris and perform comprehensive vacuuming of seats, carpets, floor mats, crevices, and hard-to-reach areas throughout your interior.

Final Detailing & Quality Check
