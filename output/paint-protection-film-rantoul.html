What is Paint Protection Film?

Paint Protection Film (PPF), also known as clear bra, is a transparent, thermoplastic urethane film that is applied to your vehicle's painted surfaces to protect them from damage. This advanced technology creates an invisible shield that guards against stone chips, scratches, bug splatter, road debris, and environmental contaminants while maintaining your vehicle's original appearance.

At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, we use only premium paint protection film from industry-leading manufacturers. Our films feature self-healing properties that eliminate minor scratches and swirl marks when exposed to heat, ensuring your vehicle maintains its pristine finish for years to come.
