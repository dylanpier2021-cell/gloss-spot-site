What is Car Wraps?

Car wraps are high-quality vinyl films applied to your vehicle's exterior surfaces to completely transform its appearance. This process involves precisely cutting and installing premium vinyl materials that can mimic any color, pattern, or finish imaginable. Unlike traditional paint jobs, car wraps in Urbana offer unlimited design possibilities while protecting your vehicle's original paint underneath.

Professional car wrapping involves removing trim pieces, carefully cleaning the surface, and using specialized tools to apply the vinyl without bubbles or imperfections. The result is a stunning transformation that can advertise your business, express your personal style, or simply protect your investment. Our Urbana team specializes in both full vehicle wraps and partial graphics, ensuring every project meets the highest standards of quality and durability.

From color changes and custom graphics to commercial fleet branding, car wraps provide versatility that traditional paint cannot match. The installation process typically takes 2-4 days depending on complexity, and when properly maintained, quality wraps can last 5-7 years in Urbana's climate while maintaining their vibrant appearance.

Our Car Wraps Process

1

Consultation & Design

We meet with you to understand your vision, take vehicle measurements, and create custom designs. Our Urbana team provides expert recommendations for materials, colors, and finishes that suit your needs and budget.

2

Vehicle Preparation

Your vehicle undergoes thorough cleaning and paint decontamination. We remove necessary trim pieces and ensure all surfaces are perfectly clean and smooth for optimal vinyl adhesion.

3

Precision Installation

Our certified technicians carefully apply the vinyl using professional-grade tools and techniques. Each panel is precisely positioned, heated, and conformed to your vehicle's curves for a seamless finish.

4

Quality Inspection

We perform a comprehensive quality check, ensuring no bubbles, wrinkles, or imperfections exist. All trim pieces are reinstalled, and your vehicle is prepared for delivery.

5

Care Instructions

We provide detailed maintenance guidelines to maximize your wrap's lifespan. Our team explains proper washing techniques and offers ongoing support for your investment.
