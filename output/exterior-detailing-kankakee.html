What is Exterior Detailing?

Exterior detailing is a comprehensive cleaning and restoration process that goes far beyond a simple car wash. Our professional exterior detailing service in Kankakee includes thorough paint decontamination, paint correction, polishing, and protection to restore your vehicle's original shine and protect it from the elements.

At The Gloss Spot Auto Detailing, our exterior detailing process involves multiple steps including pre-wash foam treatment, hand washing with premium products, clay bar decontamination to remove embedded contaminants, paint correction to eliminate swirl marks and scratches, and final protection with high-quality wax or ceramic coating. This meticulous approach ensures your vehicle looks better than the day you bought it.

Our Kankakee exterior detailing service also includes wheel and tire cleaning, chrome polishing, glass cleaning inside and out, and trim restoration. We use only professional-grade products and techniques to deliver results that last months, not days. Whether you're preparing for a special event or maintaining your vehicle's value, our exterior detailing service will exceed your expectations.

Our Exterior Detailing Process

01

Pre-Wash Inspection & Setup

We begin with a thorough inspection of your vehicle's condition and set up our professional equipment to ensure the best results for your Kankakee exterior detailing service.

02

Foam Wash & Hand Washing

Pre-wash foam treatment loosens dirt and contaminants, followed by careful hand washing using premium products and proper techniques to avoid scratching your paint.

03

Clay Bar Decontamination

We use professional clay bars to remove embedded contaminants, tree sap, and other stubborn particles that regular washing can't eliminate.

04

Paint Correction & Polishing

Our skilled technicians perform paint correction to remove swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation, then polish to restore maximum gloss and clarity.

05

Protection & Final Details

We apply premium wax or ceramic coating for long-lasting protection, clean wheels and tires, polish chrome, and ensure every detail is perfect.
