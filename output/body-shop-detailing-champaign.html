What is Body Shop Detailing?

Body shop detailing in Champaign is a specialized service designed specifically for collision repair facilities, dealerships, and insurance restoration work. Unlike regular car detailing, body shop detailing focuses on returning vehicles to pristine, showroom condition after repairs, painting, or bodywork has been completed.

Our Champaign body shop detailing service includes comprehensive cleaning and restoration of both interior and exterior surfaces, removal of dust and debris from repair work, paint polishing and protection, and detailed documentation with before and after photos for insurance purposes. We work directly with body shops throughout Champaign to ensure vehicles are delivered to customers in perfect condition.

As trusted partners with multiple Champaign area body shops, we understand the unique requirements of post-repair detailing, including paint care, adhesive removal, protective film application, and maintaining the integrity of fresh repairs while achieving that new car finish.

Inspection & Documentation

Complete vehicle assessment with detailed photos documenting condition before detailing begins. We coordinate with your Champaign body shop team on specific requirements.

2

Paint Correction & Protection
