What is Paint Correction?

Paint correction is a meticulous multi-step process that removes imperfections from your vehicle's paint surface. Using specialized compounds, polishes, and techniques, we eliminate swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, oxidation, and other defects that dull your car's appearance.

Unlike simple polishing or buffing, true paint correction in Decatur requires expert knowledge of paint systems, proper tools, and years of experience. Our certified technicians assess your vehicle's specific needs and develop a customized approach to restore that deep, mirror-like finish you remember from the showroom floor.

The process involves carefully leveling microscopic imperfections in your clear coat through controlled abrasion, followed by progressively finer polishes to achieve optical clarity. When done correctly, paint correction can restore up to 95% of your paint's original gloss and depth, making your vehicle look years younger.
