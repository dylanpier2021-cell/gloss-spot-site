What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's factory paint, creating an additional protective layer. This advanced nanotechnology forms a permanent or semi-permanent bond that cannot be washed away or broken down by chemical contaminants. In Savoy, where vehicles face harsh Illinois weather, road salt, and UV exposure, ceramic coating provides unmatched protection against environmental damage.

Unlike traditional waxes and sealants that sit on top of the paint, ceramic coatings create a molecular bond that enhances your vehicle's natural properties. The result is a hydrophobic surface that repels water, dirt, and contaminants while providing incredible depth and gloss to your paint. For Savoy vehicle owners, this means easier maintenance, better protection, and a showroom shine that lasts for years.
