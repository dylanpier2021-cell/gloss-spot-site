What is Exterior Detailing?

Exterior detailing in Bloomington is a comprehensive process that goes far beyond a simple car wash. Our professional exterior detailing services restore and protect your vehicle's paint, chrome, glass, and trim to like-new condition. This meticulous process includes thorough washing, clay bar treatment to remove contaminants, paint correction to eliminate swirl marks and scratches, polishing for maximum shine, and protective coating application.

Our Bloomington exterior detailing team uses premium products and proven techniques to address paint oxidation, UV damage, environmental contaminants, and everyday wear. Whether you need paint correction, ceramic coating, or complete exterior restoration, our car detailing services in Bloomington deliver showroom-quality results that protect your investment and enhance your vehicle's appearance for years to come.

Our Exterior Detailing Process

1

Thorough Inspection & Pre-Wash

We carefully inspect your vehicle's exterior condition and pre-rinse to remove loose dirt and debris, preparing the surface for deep cleaning.

2

Deep Wash & Decontamination

Using premium soap and the two-bucket method, we perform a thorough wash followed by clay bar treatment to remove embedded contaminants.

3

Paint Correction & Polishing

We carefully remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation using professional compounds and polishes, restoring your paint's clarity and depth.

4

Protective Coating Application

We apply high-quality wax, sealant, or ceramic coating to protect your paint and provide long-lasting shine and water beading properties.

5

Final Details & Quality Check

We clean and dress all exterior trim, tires, and glass, then perform a comprehensive quality inspection to ensure perfection.
